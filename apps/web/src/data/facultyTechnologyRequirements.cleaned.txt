Kafedra Tarixi
Since 2001-2002 academic year on the basis of Bukhara Autonomous Okrug and Yesti, the Department of Technologies of Oil and Gas Processing has been organized. The purpose of the creation of the department in this region was to provide qualified personnel to the Bukhara oil refinery, the Mubarak gas refinery, the Gazlineftegaziburization department and other enterprises. Originally a PhD in Chemistry. M. Mardonov headed the department. From 2002 to 2009, the department was headed by PhD of Technical Sciences Q.K.Jumaev. From 2009 to 2011, the department was headed by PhD of Technical Sciences J.A.Qalandarov.
On April 15, 2013, in accordance with the decree PP-1954 "On the establishment of the Bukhara Engineering and Technical Institute", the Department of "Technologies of Oil and Gas Processing" and "Oil and Gas Business" was formed from the merger of the departments "Technology of Oil and Gas Processing" and "Oil and Gas Business". From that day until January 2, 2014, the department was headed by PhD in technical sciences B.Z.Adizov. From January 2, 2014 to July 20, 2014, Ph.D. Sharipov worked as acting head of the department. From July 20, 2014 to July 10, 2019, Professor G. R. Bozorov headed the department.
On July 10, 2019, three departments ("Oil Refining Technology", "Gas Chemical Processing Technology" and "Oil and Gas Business") were established on the basis of the Department of Petroleum and Gas Chemical Industry Technology, which was approved by the Ministry of Secondary Education of the Republic of Uzbekistan. From January 28, 2022, the department was headed by Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) A.A. Alimov, and from 2023 to 2025, associate professor Sh.N. Ataullayev.
In accordance with the Decree of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan dated January 24, 2025 No. PP-22 "On the establishment of the Bukhara State Technical University" from April 10, 2025, Ph.D. R.R. Hayitov worked as the head of the department. From September 13, 2025 to the present, the department has been headed by Ph.D., associate professor. A.A.Ochilov is in charge.

Food consumption is of very important importance in human life. However, the widespread use of modern intensive technologies, the use of chemicals and other non-food additives can be one of the main causes of food spoilage, including their biochemical changes and microbial damage and poisoning.
The development of unprofitable microflora leads to spoilage of finished products, causing significant economic losses for both producers and consumers.
Certain biotechnologically and chemical methods of decontamination have microbostatic characteristics which sometimes fail to ensure the required quality of the product. Research on technological and cost-effective methods of decontamination of products in hot climates is an actual issue, has scientific and practical value.

project is aimed at studying the current importance of functional food products, their role and comparative analysis in developed countries, the development of a technology for the production of functionally oriented flour, oatmeal and bread products based on local raw materials, the differences between the developed products and rational food products and the prophylactic and therapeutic properties.

Doctoral student of our department Djuraeva N.R. conducts scientific work on the theme: "Scientific and practical aspects of targeted combination of raw materials in the production of vegetable and oil mixtures". The thesis work is the development of scientifically based recipes and resource-saving technologies of vegetable-oil composition mixtures, the biopotential of which is predicted in advance, while the rational use of natural additives with natural stabilizers that increase the physiological value of bread and bakery products, quality indicators, shelf life, save the basic raw materials. The subject of the research was the development of innovative resource-intensive technology for the production of functional plant-fat compositions using environmentally friendly plant raw materials, while the object of the research was oils and vegetable oils, flax, soybean products, emulsion and anhydrous oil-and-paste mixtures, bread and flour products.
The department conducts scientific work by the basic doctoral student Muzafarova H.M. on the theme: "The use of unconventional raw materials in the development of new recipes for flour confectionery products for heroditic nutrition." In the development and creation of functional foods for herodietary purposes, one should know the chemical composition, nutritional value and special processing techniques of raw materials. In this context, the urgent task facing all branches of the food industry is to expand the range of products of food for herodietic purpose and improve technology through the use of natural, affordable raw materials and materials that meet the requirements of nutrition. The thesis proposes an approach to solutions, consisting of practical recommendations for the use of unconventional raw materials in the development of new recipes for bakery products for herodietic nutrition, which provides an increase in biotechnological potential, sets scientific tasks and substantiates it.
Assistant of the department Ismatova Sh.N. conducts scientific work on the theme: "Rational use of non-traditional means in feed feed". The purpose of the study is to improve the technology of production of feed with high nutritional value using unconventional feed means. The objectives of the research are to analyze and systematize modern data on improving productivity and reproductive qualities of poultry when using unconventional feed and feed additives, to select and substantiate raw material components for feed in the feed, to study their chemical composition, functional and technological properties and nutritional safety, to study the dried quinoa and amaranth grains (seeds) It consists of the development of technological parameters for unconventional methods of preparation and determination of the optimal dosage for their inclusion in the diet of chickens, as well as the study of the growth and development of poultry, meatiness, egg productivity and reproductive properties when using feed with added unconventional additives.
