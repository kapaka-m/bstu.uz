# University Administrative Departments & Centers

The following centers and departments are officially configured in the BSTU International portal.

This file is a documentation summary only. The public website source of truth is
the Laravel/MySQL CMS data, managed from `/apanel/`; do not copy these entries
into React source files.

## Administrative Centers

1. **Digital Educational Technologies Centre**: Manages campus-wide network, software licenses, computer labs, and portals.
2. **Inclusive IT Center**: Focuses on assistive IT training, music therapy, and accessible technologies.
3. **Professional Development and Retraining Center**: Offers continuing education and vocational retraining for specialists.
4. **Information Resource Center**: The central digital and physical library catalog of the university.

## University Departments

1. **Сhancellery and Archival Department**: Coordinates formal university document flows.
2. **Monitoring and Inner Control Department**: Reviews internal policies and educational standards.
3. **Human Resources Department**: Manages academic staff and administration recruitment.
4. **Education Quality Control Department**: Audits curriculum delivery and student learning satisfaction.
5. **Trade Union Committee**: Represents employee welfare and student unions.
6. **Educational and Methodical Department**: Standardizes lecture plans and exam procedures.
7. **Master’s Department**: Oversees postgraduate and master's degree specializations.
8. **Department of Youth Affairs, Spirituality, and Enlightenment**: Promotes student extracurricular activities and national values.
9. **Department for Organizing Scientific Research Activities of Talented Students**: Coordinates student research grants.
10. **International Cooperation Department**: Manages partner university relations and exchange programs.
11. **Anti-Corruption Compliance Control System Management Department**: Directs transparency and compliance.
12. **Department of Civil and Labor Protection**: Oversees campus occupational health and emergency drills.
13. **Legal Service Department**: Offers counsel on agreements and structural charters.
14. **Department of Scientific Research, Innovations, and Training of Scientific-Pedagogical Staff**: Manages postgraduate degrees.
15. **Vocational Education Coordination Department**: Connects vocational schools with university pipelines.
