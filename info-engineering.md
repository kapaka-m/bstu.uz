Faculty of Engineering

Currently, the Faculty of Engineering educates 1,161 students across 60 academic groups. Of these, 916 study on a contract basis, while 245 students study under the State Grant. Among them, 958 are male students and 203 are female students.
The faculty has 6 departments, 4 of which are specialized departments. At present, 111 academic staff members are working at the Faculty of Engineering, including: 8 Professors, 26 Associate Professors, 30 Senior Lecturers, 15 Assistants, and 32 Trainee Lecturers. In addition, 4 foreign professors are part of the faculty.
Following the 5 key initiatives of the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan to enhance youth spirituality and organize their free time meaningfully, the faculty has established over 20 scientific and technical clubs, as well as a creative-artistic group called “Satire and Humor.”
The faculty has 3 Beruni State Scholarship holders and 1 I. Karimov State Scholarship holder. Among the students, 10 have won international competitions, and 12 have won national competitions and science olympiads. Furthermore, students and academic staff regularly participate in sports competitions organized by the institute and have consistently won prizes.
A Scientific Innovation Center has been established at the faculty and is currently active. The center conducts scientific research while also facilitating economic contracts and student employment. At present, 10 students and 8 research staff are employed there full-time.
Two faculty teams regularly participate in the “Zakovat” intellectual games organized at the institute. The “Satire and Humor” team won an honorable 3rd place in April and received valuable prizes. In student football competitions, the faculty team secured 2nd place, in mini-football 3rd place, and among academic staff, 3rd place.
To provide students with meaningful extracurricular activities, the faculty has organized 3 clubs based on student interests (“Satire and Humor” Student Theater Studio, “Architectural Industrialization” QVZ Team, Zakovat Group), as well as 15 clubs and sports sections.
Additionally, group tutors have organized mini-football competitions among student groups.

FACULTY OF ENGINEERING
The Faculty of Engineering-is an academic division of a higher education institution that prepares highly qualified specialists in various engineering fields. Students gain both theoretical knowledge and practical skills, studying modern technologies, design, manufacturing processes, and innovative solutions. The faculty plays a key role in training competent professionals for industry, construction, energy, and other technical sectors.
